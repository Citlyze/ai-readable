Acme Widget Pro buying guide

What is the Acme Widget Pro?

The Acme Widget Pro is a mid-range widget for small workshops. It pairs a reinforced housing with a two-year warranty, and in our tests it carried about forty percent more load than the base model without extra noise or heat.

How much does it cost?

Pricing starts at 129 dollars for the base configuration and rises to 199 dollars with the extended battery, which most buyers can skip unless they work off-grid for more than six hours at a time on a typical day.

Which model should a small workshop pick?

Pick the base model unless you run the widget away from mains power. The extended battery adds weight, and the base model already covers a full shift in most workshops we spoke to during testing.
