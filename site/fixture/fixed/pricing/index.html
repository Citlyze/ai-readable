Acme Widget Pro pricing plans

How much does the Acme Widget Pro cost?

The base model costs 129 dollars and the extended-battery model costs 199 dollars. Both include a two-year warranty, free shipping inside the country, and a thirty-day return window with no restocking fee for unused units.

Which plan is right for a small workshop?

Most workshops pick the base model because it covers a full shift on mains power. Choose the extended battery only if you work off-grid for more than six hours at a time, since it adds weight and cost you will not otherwise use.
