Acme Widget Pro accessories catalogue

Which accessories fit the Widget Pro?

Every accessory made for the base model fits the extended-battery model too, because both share the same housing and mounting rail. The only exception is the slim carry case, which does not close over the larger battery.

Where can I buy accessories?

Accessories ship from the same store as the widget itself, and orders placed with a widget ship together at no extra cost. Retail partners carry the most common items, but the full range is online only.

  • Spare filters
  • Mounting rail
