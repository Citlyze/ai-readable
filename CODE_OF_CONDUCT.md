# Code of conduct

Be useful and be kind. This project is maintained by a small team and used by people who are often frustrated that their site is invisible to AI search. Help them fix it.

- Assume good faith. A wrong result is a bug report, not an argument.
- Review the work, not the person. No harassment, no discrimination, no personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or anywhere the project is discussed.
- Keep it on topic. Marketing pitches, unrelated tool promotion and spam are removed.
- Respect scope decisions. Maintainers may close pull requests that fall outside the project's purpose, with thanks.

Report unacceptable behaviour to hello@citlyze.com. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts that break these rules.

Adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
